Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 2026

1 Who We Are

ViralShelf is a digital products business based in Ontario, California, United States. We sell SVG cut files and digital design assets through Etsy and other digital marketplaces. This privacy policy explains how ViralShelf collects, uses, and protects your information.

This policy applies to ViralShelf's website at viralshelf.netlify.app and all services operated under the ViralShelf brand.

2 Information We Collect

ViralShelf may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information you voluntarily provide (e.g., name, email address when contacting us)
  • Usage data collected automatically when you visit our site (e.g., browser type, pages visited, time on site)
  • Pinterest data accessed through the Pinterest API, limited to public board and pin data necessary to distribute ViralShelf content
  • Purchase data processed by Etsy on our behalf — ViralShelf does not directly collect payment information

3 How We Use Your Information

ViralShelf uses collected information to:

  • Deliver and improve our digital products and services
  • Respond to customer inquiries and support requests
  • Distribute product content on Pinterest and other social platforms via authorized API integrations
  • Analyze website traffic to improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4 Pinterest API Usage

ViralShelf uses the Pinterest API to automatically publish pins of our digital products (SVG cut files and design assets) to our official Pinterest account @ViralShelf.

  • We access only the Pinterest permissions necessary to create and manage pins on our own account
  • We do not access, store, or use any Pinterest user's private data
  • We do not post to or access any Pinterest account other than our own
  • Pinterest data accessed via the API is used solely for distributing ViralShelf product listings
ViralShelf complies with Pinterest's Developer Terms of Service and API usage policies.

5 Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. We may use standard analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site.

6 Data Sharing & Third Parties

ViralShelf works with the following third-party services to operate our business:

  • Etsy — sales platform and payment processing
  • Pinterest — content distribution via official API
  • Netlify — website hosting
  • Airtable — internal data management (not customer-facing)

Each of these services has their own privacy policies governing their data practices. ViralShelf does not share personal data beyond what is necessary to operate these integrations.

7 Data Retention & Security

ViralShelf retains personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We implement reasonable security measures to protect information under our control. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications
  • California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address below.

9 Children's Privacy

ViralShelf's website and services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10 Changes to This Policy

ViralShelf may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
